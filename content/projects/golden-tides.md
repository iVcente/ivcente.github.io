*Golden Tides* is a 4v4 adventure MOBA with WASD-controlled combat and no lanes or towers. Each match takes place across three islands, chosen at random from a pool of maps, with ship-to-ship naval combat between them in which cannon shots work as skillshots. On the first island, both crews fight NPCs and each other while searching for a buried treasure map, and the crew that finds it sets sail with a lead. The second island holds a single treasure that a crew must find and carry back to its ship before the other crew can take it. On the final island, the crews fight to deliver the treasure to a delivery point, which wins the match. Throughout the match, players earn gold to spend on items at shops.

I joined the project during its preparation phase for a public release. My primary responsibilities were general gameplay bugfixing, along with refactoring, improving, and optimizing existing systems, with a focus on networking and CPU performance. I also implemented UI systems.
